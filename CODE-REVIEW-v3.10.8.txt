Angler's Jigsaw v3.10.8 — Toolbar Reliability Review

Observed architecture in v3.10.7:
- Gameplay controls used delegated pointerup plus a click fallback.
- Clicks within 450 ms of a pointer action were deliberately ignored to prevent duplicates.
- iPadOS can cancel or fail to return pointerup after fullscreen/system/drag transitions.
- Therefore a physical tap could produce no action, requiring repeated presses.
- PUSH/PULL also returned immediately while boardActionBusy was true, silently discarding a press.

v3.10.8 corrections:
1. Single delegated pointerdown handler for every gameplay toolbar action.
2. Click handler is keyboard/accessibility only (event.detail === 0).
3. PUSH/PULL queue the newest request if the board is busy rather than dropping it.
4. Toolbar has explicit z-index 200 and pointer-events/touch-action rules.
5. Play table, tray and drag layers remain beneath the toolbar.
6. No changes to the ARTEZIQ UI/audio modules.
