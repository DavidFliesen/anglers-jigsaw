Quick guide

How to Play

  1. Choose a fish and puzzle size.
  2. Move pieces from the tray to the table. Use Edges to Table or All to Table whenever you want.
  3. Build it your way. Sort by edge, color, shape, or make your own piles. Correct pieces snap when placed in their proper location.
  4. Use Preview when needed. Finish the puzzle to add the species to Fish Caught.
