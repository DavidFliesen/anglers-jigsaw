Choose Puzzle Difficulty

Every level uses the same 4:3 puzzle board. Higher levels divide it into more interlocking pieces.

How to Play

  1. Choose a difficulty based on the number of puzzle pieces.
  2. A fish is selected from the Angler's Jigsaw collection.
  3. Use Edges Only to work the border first, or switch to All Pieces.
  4. Drag pieces from the Tackle Tray onto the puzzle table.
  5. Bring matching edges close together and they will snap into a connected group.
  6. Connected groups move together until the entire puzzle is complete.
  7. Finish the puzzle to learn about the species and add it to your Fish Cooler.
