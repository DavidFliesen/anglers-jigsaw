How to Play

Pick a fish, choose your puzzle size, then solve it your way.

  1. 1
    Choose a puzzle

    Select a fish and difficulty.

  2. 2
    Spread out the pieces

    Drag pieces from the tray, or use Edges to Table or All to Table.

  3. 3
    Build the picture

    Move pieces wherever you want. Correct pieces snap and lock when they reach their spot.

  4. 4
    Finish the catch

    Complete the puzzle to add the species to your Fish Cooler.
