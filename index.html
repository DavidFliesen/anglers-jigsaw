How to Play

  1. Choose a body of water.
  2. A fish from that habitat is selected for your puzzle.
  3. Drag traditional interlocking jigsaw pieces from the Tackle Tray onto the board.
  4. Pieces snap into place when they are close to the correct position.
  5. Use Edges Only if you like starting with the border.
  6. Complete the fish to learn about the species and add it to your Fish Cooler.
