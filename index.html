Quick guide

How to Play

  1. Start at Level 1. Before each level, choose 12, 48, 108, or 192 pieces.
  2. Build anywhere. Correct neighboring pieces magnetically snap together and move as a connected group.
  3. Use the simple controls. ALL/EDGE changes the tray view, To Board sends visible tray pieces to the table, Pull Loose returns only unconnected pieces, and Trace shows the reference image.
  4. Finish the fish. A correctly assembled piece or group snaps into its board position. Complete the puzzle to identify the species and unlock the next level.
