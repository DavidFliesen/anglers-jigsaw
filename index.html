How to Play

  1. Choose a body of water.
  2. Tap a fishing hotspot to cast your line.
  3. When you get a bite, reel the fish in before it gets away.
  4. The catch transforms into a puzzle.
  5. Drag pieces from the Tackle Tray onto the board. Tap-to-select also works for accessibility.
  6. Use Edges Only if you like starting with the border.
  7. Complete the fish to finish the level and add it to your Fish Cooler.
