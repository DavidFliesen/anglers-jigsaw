How to Play

Large Play Area

The tabletop is kept as open as possible so you can see pieces clearly, spread them out, and play in a way that feels natural.

Start Your Way

All pieces start in the bottom Tackle Tray. Use Edges to Table to scatter edge pieces first, or All to Table to push everything out and start exploring.

Traditional Puzzle Feel

Pieces use a classic ribbon-cut jigsaw style with deeper tabs and blanks so they feel like real puzzle pieces instead of simplified blocks.

Snap Into Place

When a piece is close enough to its correct spot on the puzzle, it snap-locks into place. Locked pieces stay put.

Use the Tray

You can drag a piece out of the tray or drag a loose piece back into the tray when you want to clear space and rethink your approach.

Preview and Full Screen

Turn on a preview when you want a quick reminder of the full image, or go full screen for the biggest playing area possible.
